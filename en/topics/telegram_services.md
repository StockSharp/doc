# Telegram Services

Telegram integration is available for remote management of trading robots, for example when they run on virtual servers.

This integration works with both ready-made programs (such as [Designer](designer.md)) and your own software developed using the [API](api.md).

Two services are available:

- [Alerts](telegram_services/alerts.md) - receive notifications from a trading robot.
- [Control Panel](telegram_services/control_panel.md) - start, stop, and control trading robots through a Telegram bot.
