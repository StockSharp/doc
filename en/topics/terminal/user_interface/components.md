# Components

During trading, you need to see the market, your orders and positions. For this, graphical components are added to the [workspace](workspace.md) — with the buttons of the vertical strip at the right edge of the area. Each click opens one more panel of this kind.

![Terminal component buttons](../../../images/terminal_components_00.png)

- [Instruments](components/instruments.md) - a table with instruments that displays information about all selected instruments.
- [Level 1](components/level_1.md) - a table with a history of changing Level 1 for the selected instruments.
- [Buy\/Sell](components/buy_sell.md) - a group of tickets that show the best prices for the instruments and with which orders are placed and cancelled.
- [Order book](components/order_book.md) - a table of limit orders for purchase and sale.
- [Chart](components/chart.md) - candles and indicators for the selected instrument; you can also trade from the chart.
- [P&L equity](../../designer/user_interface/components/pnl_equity.md) - a chart of profit and loss (unrealized and realized) and commission.
- [Own trades](../../designer/user_interface/components/trades.md) - a table of own trades.
- [Orders](components/orders.md) - a table of own orders; above it are the buttons for cancelling orders and the choice of which orders to show.
- [Positions](components/positions.md) - a table of current positions; above it are the buttons for closing and reversing a position.
- [Tick trades](../../designer/user_interface/components/tick_trades.md) - a table of all trades of the venue for the selected instruments.
- [Positions (chart)](../../designer/user_interface/components/positions.md) - a chart of the position.
- **Properties** - the properties of the object selected in the area: an instrument, an order, a trade, a position.
- [Positions (options)](components/positions_options.md) - a graphic display of the position by options.
- [Option desk](components/option_desk.md) - a table of the main parameters of options for the underlying instrument.
- [Smile volatility](components/smile_volatility.md) - the expected volatility of options with the same underlying asset and different strikes.
- [Order log](components/order_log.md) - a table of all orders of the venue for the selected instruments.
- [Level 2](components/level_2.md) - the order book of one instrument in two tables with a ticket under them.

The panels of the whole window — [Logs](logs.md), [Portfolios](portfolios.md), [Board editor](board_editor.md), **Market data**, [News](components/news.md) and [Risks](risk_management.md) — are opened from the **View** menu of the [main menu](ribbon.md).

The tables of all components work the same way: how to copy them, search in them, filter, sort and configure the columns is described in the [Working with tables](tables.md) section.
