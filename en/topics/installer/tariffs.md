# Subscription plans

The **Subscription plans** page shows which plans are available to the account. Unlike the other pages, it is opened not with a tab but with an offer at the top of the [Installer](../installer.md) window — and while it is shown, the tab bar is hidden.

Each plan shows what it includes and how much it costs. The **Subscribe** button opens the checkout on the website in the browser — the purchase happens there, not in the program. The **Postpone the choice** button closes the page and returns to what was on the screen.

The free plan is selected here as well and does not require payment.

After the purchase, the rights do not appear instantly: return to the [Licenses](licenses.md) page and click **Refresh license** — [Installer](../installer.md) will request them from the server again.
