# MATLAB

![matlab stocksharp](../images/matlab_stocksharp.png)

**MATLAB** is designed for traders who develop trading algorithms within the [MATLAB MathWorks](https://www.mathworks.com/) environment. 

The **MATLAB** integration connector allows users to connect to all brokers and exchanges supported by [S#](api.md) (+30 connection types across global markets). MATLAB scripts can receive real-time market data and send trading orders. 

Detailed settings, scripts, and documentation are available to customers who have purchased **MATLAB**.
